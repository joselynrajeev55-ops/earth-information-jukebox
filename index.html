Earth Information Jukebox

Track 1: Arctic sea ice

Press play. Low note = lots of ice. High note = less ice.
How to listen
Each note is one year of Arctic sea ice, measured every September when the ice is at its smallest. A low note means more ice. A high note means less ice. Listen to the pitch climb as the ice disappears.

Data: NSIDC Sea Ice Index (NOAA at NSIDC), based on NASA satellite passive microwave data.
